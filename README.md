# KidsKo 🚀

**KidsKo is an AI-powered voice learning companion designed to make learning more interactive, engaging, and natural for children.**

KidsKo allows children to interact with an AI learning companion through natural voice conversations, ask questions, explore different topics, and receive engaging educational responses.

> 🚧 **Project Status: Under Active Development**

KidsKo is currently being developed and tested. Features and functionality are continuously evolving as the project moves toward a production-ready experience.

---

## 🌟 About KidsKo

KidsKo aims to create a more natural approach to digital learning by combining **voice interaction and artificial intelligence**.

Instead of relying only on traditional lessons, menus, and text-based interactions, KidsKo is designed around conversational learning.

A child can simply speak, ask a question, and interact with the AI learning companion naturally.

### Example

**Child:**

> Why is the sky blue?

**KidsKo:**

> The sky looks blue because sunlight interacts with Earth's atmosphere. Blue light gets scattered more than most other colors, making the sky appear blue to us.

The goal is to make learning feel more like **talking with a helpful learning buddy**.
